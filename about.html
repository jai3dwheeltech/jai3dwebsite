About Jai 3D Wheeltech

Trusted car care, wheel and tyre services since 2015.

Our Story

Jai 3D Wheeltech has been serving customers since 2015, providing professional automobile care, wheel and tyre services in Banswada.

Over the years, we have grown with our customers by focusing on quality products, reliable service and professional workmanship. Our aim is to provide customers with a convenient destination for their vehicle's tyre, wheel and car care requirements.

Our journey has also included partnerships with leading tyre brands. We became an authorised Bridgestone dealership in 2021 and added Yokohama dealership in 2024.

Today, Jai 3D Wheeltech provides a range of services including car washing, foam wash, wheel alignment, tyres and exterior car care services for customers in Banswada and surrounding areas of Kamareddy district.

What We Do

From routine car washing and foam wash to tyre replacement and wheel alignment, our services are designed to help customers take care of their vehicles at one convenient location.

We work with both everyday vehicle owners and customers looking for professional tyre and wheel solutions, with a focus on proper service, quality products and customer satisfaction.

Our Commitment

At Jai 3D Wheeltech, we believe good service is about more than just completing a job. We aim to understand the customer's requirement, recommend suitable solutions and provide dependable workmanship.

Our goal is to continue building long-term relationships with customers through honest service, quality products and consistent care for their vehicles.
