Benefits of Proper Alignment

Correct wheel alignment can help maintain proper vehicle handling and reduce unnecessary and uneven tyre wear.

  • Helps reduce uneven tyre wear
  • Helps improve steering response
  • Helps maintain vehicle stability
  • Helps maintain proper tyre contact
  • Supports a smoother driving experience

When Should You Check Wheel Alignment?

Wheel alignment should be checked if your vehicle pulls to one side, the steering wheel is not centred while driving straight, or the tyres show uneven or abnormal wear.

It is also recommended to check alignment after hitting potholes or kerbs, after fitting new tyres, or after steering and suspension related work.

Regular alignment inspection can help identify problems early and may help prevent excessive or uneven tyre wear.
