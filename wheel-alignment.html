Professional 3D Wheel Alignment Service

Proper wheel alignment is important for maintaining vehicle stability, steering control, tyre life and driving comfort. At Jai 3D Wheeltech, we provide professional 3D wheel alignment services for different types of vehicles.

Our alignment process helps inspect the vehicle's wheel geometry and identify alignment deviations. The alignment readings are checked against the applicable vehicle specifications and necessary adjustments are carried out wherever possible.

What We Check

  • Complete wheel alignment inspection
  • Front and rear wheel alignment, wherever applicable
  • Steering wheel and steering-centre check
  • Camber, caster and toe readings, wherever applicable
  • Tyre condition and uneven tyre wear inspection
  • Before-and-after alignment readings
  • Steering and vehicle handling observations

When Should You Get Wheel Alignment?

Wheel alignment should be checked when your vehicle pulls to one side, the steering wheel is not centred while driving straight, or the tyres show uneven or abnormal wear.

It is also advisable to check alignment after driving through potholes or hitting kerbs, after fitting new tyres, or following steering and suspension-related repairs.

Benefits of Proper Wheel Alignment

  • Helps reduce uneven and premature tyre wear
  • Helps improve steering response and vehicle stability
  • Provides a more comfortable driving experience
  • Helps maintain proper tyre contact with the road
  • Helps reduce unnecessary tyre and suspension stress
  • Helps maintain better driving confidence
