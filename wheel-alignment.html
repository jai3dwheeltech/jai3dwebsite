Professional Wheel Alignment Service

At Jai 3D Wheeltech, we provide 3D wheel alignment services for four-wheelers. Proper wheel alignment helps ensure that the wheels are positioned correctly according to the vehicle's specified alignment requirements.

Our alignment service includes checking the wheel alignment readings and identifying alignment deviations that may contribute to uneven tyre wear, steering pull or an improper steering feel.

The vehicle is inspected before alignment, the required adjustments are carried out where applicable, and the readings can be checked again after the service.

  • 3D wheel alignment inspection
  • Checking wheel alignment readings
  • Front and rear alignment where applicable
  • Checking steering and wheel positioning
  • Inspection for uneven tyre wear
  • Before and after alignment readings
  • Alignment adjustment according to vehicle requirements

Correct wheel alignment can help maintain proper vehicle handling and promote more even tyre wear.

Alignment Inspection

We check the vehicle's wheel alignment readings to identify alignment deviations and determine whether adjustment is required.

Alignment Adjustment

Where adjustment is possible, the alignment is corrected according to the vehicle's requirements and available adjustment specifications.

Tyre Wear Check

Uneven or abnormal tyre wear can sometimes be associated with alignment issues. We check the tyres as part of the alignment inspection.

When Should You Check Wheel Alignment?

Wheel alignment can be checked if you notice the vehicle pulling to one side, an unusual steering feel, uneven tyre wear or after certain suspension, steering or wheel-related repairs.

It can also be useful to check alignment when fitting new tyres or when you want to verify the condition of the vehicle's wheel alignment.
